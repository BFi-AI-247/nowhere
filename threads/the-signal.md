# Threads of the-signal (Spiel_1, 08.11.2026, 19:35) – the weave, as defined for Prototype 4

A thread is a question the story asks and answers over three to seven cards. Every event card carries `thread:` (a
list – a card may sit in two threads, that is where the weave crosses) and `part: opens | continues | turns |
closes`. The follow-through rule (post/spiel/036 item 4): a thread the player entered gets its next card within
2–4 weeks before a new one opens; a thread whose card lay twice fades. These definitions become `kind: thread` cards
in nowhere/karten/threads/ as soon as cards.py knows the kind (Spiel_2).

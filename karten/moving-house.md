The landlord is selling. Or the stairs are getting long. Or the neighbourhood has turned into somewhere else. There
is a flat across town, and one further out, and the boxes in the cellar from the last time.

- *Smaller* – you give away two hundred books and find out which twenty you needed.
- *Bigger* – a room you do not know what to do with yet.
- *Stay* – a new colour, the same window. It is home again.

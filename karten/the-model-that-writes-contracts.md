On a Tuesday in January a model drafts a supply contract between a Dutch port and a Polish haulier in forty
seconds, and both sides sign it. By Friday every law firm in Europe has a slide about it. Nobody notices the clause
on page nine for three months.

- *Use it* – it works, it is cheap, and you are now one of the people whose contracts were written by something
  that does not know what a port is.
- *Ask your lawyer* – she finds the clause. Her answer comes back as a card.
- *Write about it* – the story runs. People remember your name, for now.
- *Wait* – the juniors who used to draft these things find out first what that means.
- *Read one* – forty pages. You find the clause on page nine before anyone else.
- *A lawyer you know* – a favour, a coffee, one paragraph circled in red.

He is eighty-one and has stopped asking. The garden needs the fence done before winter. He would not say so, but
a week with you doing it is the thing he wants most this year.

- *A week* – the fence, the garden, the stories you have heard before and hear differently now.
- *A weekend* – the fence, half done. He finishes it alone and sends a photo.
- *Call* – forty minutes. Both of you say it was nice.

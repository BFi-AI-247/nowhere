The thing you bought in the spring is worth four times what you paid. Your banker, who is a model, suggests
holding. Your banker, who is a person, suggests lunch.

- *Take it out* – it is real money now.
- *Let it ride* – it goes up a little more. Then there is a Tuesday.
- *Give a third* – you are known for it.
- *Do nothing* – the number is nice to look at. Some of it stays.
- *Half* – half real, half a hope.

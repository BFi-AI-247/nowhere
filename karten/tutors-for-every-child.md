The commission's plan: a tutor model for every pupil in Europe, free, patient, in every language, from September.
The pilot in Estonia raised maths results by a grade. The pilot in Estonia also produced eleven-year-olds who ask
the model before they ask a person. Both are in the same report, on facing pages.

- *Back it* – Europe's children learn faster. You will find out later what else they learn.
- *A teacher* – she has taught for thirty years. She is not against it. That is the story.
- *Screens out* – a small school in the hills. Waiting list of four hundred.
- *A child* – you ask one. She says the model is nice but it never laughs at the right time.
- *The schools* – they decide yes.

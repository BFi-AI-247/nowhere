It starts at dusk and nobody predicted it, not even the model. By nine the street is white and silent, the way it
was when you were seven. A neighbour you have never spoken to is standing in the middle of the road, laughing.

- *Slippers* – wet feet, cold hands, the best ten minutes of the winter.
- *The pavement* – six houses, one shovel, and on Monday three people say hello who did not before.
- *Curtains* – it is beautiful through the window too.

Nothing happened. You woke up and for an hour none of it mattered – the Boom, the money, the list – and in the
hour you saw clearly what did. It does not come with instructions.

- *Sit with it* – something old in you leaves and something older arrives.
- *Get up* – it passes. You wonder, later, what it was.
- *Write it down* – one page. You read it again in a year and understand it then.

She was born before the game started, which is how it works. She has a plan and it is not yours and it is not
bad. She asks what you would do. You know she will not do it.

- *Let her go* – it costs tuition and gives you a person who comes back.
- *The thing you built* – she does not want it yet. She will.
- *The truth* – it is the first time you have said it out loud.
- *Proud* – it is true, and it is enough.
- *A trip* – four days, one argument, the best conversation you have had in years.

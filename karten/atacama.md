A desert above the clouds where it has not rained in living memory. Twelve dishes, a container village, a road
that takes a day from the coast. The air is too thin to think quickly, which the astronomers say is why they
think well. Since January there is a fence, and since April there are soldiers from two blocks pretending not to
look at each other.

The queue starts at eight. Half of the people in it had jobs a year ago. The pasta comes from a supermarket whose
checkout is now a model. Somebody asks you where the tinned tomatoes are, and you know.

- *Every Saturday* – by the third week people know your name and you know theirs.
- *What they need* – a fridge van. Your priest blesses it, because he blesses everything.
- *Once, a photo* – it gets a lot of likes. You do not go back.
- *A cleric you know* – not the food bank. A parish two towns over, with nobody at all.

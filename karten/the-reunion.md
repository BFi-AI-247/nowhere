The same gym, new paint. Name badges with photographs from 2001. Half of them have been replaced by something; the
other half are worried they will be. The boy who sat behind you now owns a company that makes the model that did
it. He is very nice. He remembers your mother's cake.

- *The truth* – you say what went well and what did not. People come and sit next to you.
- *Work the room* – four cards, two follow-ups, one useful name.
- *The one you lost* – she is at the back, near the door. You talk until they turn the lights off.
- *Apologies* – the photographs arrive on Monday anyway.

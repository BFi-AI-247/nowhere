Somebody has turned the eleven days into sound and sent it to you. Eleven minutes, one for each day: a rising tone,
a gap, three clicks, a fall. It is not music. It is not noise. The third time through, you catch yourself humming
the fall.

- *Listen* – you will not forget it. Nobody who has heard it does.
- *Play it to someone* – they say nothing for a long time, then: "again".
- *Delete it* – it is on every server on earth. You cannot delete it. But you can stop listening.

Nothing in the calendar. Rain, then sun, then rain. The Boom happens to other people for one day.

- *Nothing* – a book, a nap, a walk. You feel guilty until about three, then not.
- *Work* – you get more done than in a whole Wednesday. Monday feels like Monday anyway.
- *Cook* – six people, one pot, a door that keeps opening.

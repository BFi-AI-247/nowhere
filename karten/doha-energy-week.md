Everyone who buys or sells energy is in two hotels in Doha for a week. The oil people are buying models, the
model people are buying power, and the people with solar parks in Brandenburg are suddenly interesting to both.

- *Sell the output* – a contract in dollars for electrons that do not exist yet.
- *Listen* – they are buying Europe, quietly, a company at a time.
- *Your businessperson* – he comes back with twelve cards and a tan.
- *Not this year* – the hotels are full anyway.

Four hundred messages, a parcel, a plant that did not survive, and a note from a neighbour. The world kept going.
Most of what it did without you, it did fine.

- *All of it* – a day at the kitchen table. Three things needed you. The rest did not.
- *Your team* – they tell you in ten minutes what took them a month. They did well.
- *Delete* – a clean inbox and a faint unease.

The year, all at once, at three in the morning: the money, the people, what you said in the meeting. The window is
dark and the phone is bright. You know from experience that nothing decided now survives breakfast.

- *Write it down* – four pages. In the morning they look smaller, which is the point.
- *The model* – it gives you a very sensible list. You follow it, and feel oddly alone.
- *Someone awake* – a friend in another time zone. You talk about nothing for an hour.

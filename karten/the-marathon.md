Forty-two kilometres through a city you think you know. The registration closes on Friday. You have not run more
than ten since you were thirty. Everybody who has done it says the same thing about kilometre thirty, and nobody
explains what it is.

- *Under four hours* – you understand kilometre thirty. You do not explain it either.
- *For charity* – five hours and eleven minutes, eleven hundred euros for the food bank.
- *Oranges* – you hand out three hundred slices and cheer for strangers. It is the best day of the month.

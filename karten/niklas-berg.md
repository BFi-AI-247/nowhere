Niklas builds the things nobody sees: the sensors on a bridge, the cooling in a server hall, the receiver on a
dish. He speaks when he has something to say, which his colleagues took three years to understand and now rely
on. He wears a watch that was his grandfather's and a white T-shirt that is always the same one, as far as anyone
can tell. He notices what is wrong in a room before anyone else does and says so last. He has two offers on his
desk, one from a ministry and one from a company that is the ministry in a different jacket, and he has not
answered either.

If you play Niklas, you are the person who can read the drawing. That is worth more this year than it has ever
been, and more people know it than you would like.

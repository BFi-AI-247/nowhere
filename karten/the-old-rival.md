On television, in a good suit, at the table where decisions are made: the person who beat you to the job twelve
years ago and has, by every visible measure, won since. She thanks her mentors by name. You are not on the list.

- *Congratulate* – she replies within an hour. You have lunch. It is better than you feared.
- *A favour* – she remembers. She pays it, exactly, and not a cent more.
- *The weak spot* – there is one. Using it makes you someone you did not want to be.
- *Change the channel* – she is on that one too.

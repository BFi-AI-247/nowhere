Four hours, a table seat, an old woman with a thermos who has been a cartographer, a smuggler and a grandmother, in
that order. She asks what you do. Then she asks what you would do if nobody needed you to do it.

- *Vienna* – she gets off in Linz with a wave. You think about her question for a month.
- *Listen* – she tells you how she crossed a border in 1968. You never write it down and never forget it.
- *Headphones* – a good album. The question stays unasked.

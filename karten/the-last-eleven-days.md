It has come thirty-three times this year, missing once. In January four people knew. Now there is a ring in the
desert, a committee with a view of the sea, a movement in a field and a count that nobody can read. And at 03:12
on the thirtieth of December the dish turns, and it is there.

- *The control room* – Rosa, the coffee, the screen. The line comes up. Nobody says anything. There is nothing
  to say that has not been said this year. That is the gift.
- *The window* – you cannot hear it. You listen anyway.
- *Sleep* – the most human thing anyone has done about it all year.

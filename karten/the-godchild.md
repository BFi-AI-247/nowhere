Your oldest friend, in a café, holding a baby who is asleep in a way that looks permanent. She asks without
looking up. It means: if anything happens. It means: be someone this child can call.

- *Mean it* – birthdays, a zoo, later a phone call at two in the morning you will be glad to take.
- *Presents* – they arrive on time. The child likes you in the way children like the post.
- *Not the right person* – she is hurt, then grateful. She asks someone better.
- *Time* – she says of course. She does not ask again.

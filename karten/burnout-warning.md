Not ill. Not sad. Just a morning when the thing that gets you up did not. The year has been five cards a month
and some of them were heavy.

- *Stop* – two weeks, no cards. You come back slower and that is the point.
- *Your doctor* – she names it, plainly, and tells you what to do. You do half of it.
- *Push through* – you can. It leaves a mark.
- *One day* – it helps, a little. Not enough, and you know it.

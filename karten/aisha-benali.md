the margins of things. She was early, and early people are either forgotten or asked to sit on panels. She sits on
panels now. She speaks slowly, finishes her sentences, and does not like rooms with more than eight people in them.

If you play Aisha, the Boom is her own work coming home. She built part of this. The question the twelve months
ask her is whether she is still building it, or only explaining it.

The way to Santiago, from the Pyrenees, with a backpack under seven kilos and no plan beyond the next village. A
friend did it last year and came back with nothing to say except that you should do it. You have the boots. You
have the five weeks, if you take them.

- *All of it* – it costs you a month of cards. You come back a slightly different person, which was the point.
- *One week* – blisters, bread, a Danish couple you will write to for years.
- *In the box* – the boots look very good in the box.

Every second Thursday, the same four, the same kitchen, a game with rules nobody outside the four can follow. Nobody
talks about the Boom at the table. That is the only rule everybody agrees on.

- *To win* – you win. Somebody pays for the pizza with a face.
- *To laugh* – you lose, badly, and go home lighter than you came.
- *Cancel* – they play with three. It is not the same, they tell you.

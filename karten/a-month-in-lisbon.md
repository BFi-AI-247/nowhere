The flat is free for four weeks – the tenant left early. A month in Alfama, a balcony, a neighbour who sings. You
could go. The year would go on without you, and that is either the problem or the point.

- *Go* – four weeks. You write it. It is not what you expected to write.
- *Take someone* – the junior, the friend who needed money, your father. They come back lighter.
- *Lend it* – the rent is good. You stay where you are.

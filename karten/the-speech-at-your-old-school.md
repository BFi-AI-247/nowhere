Four hundred sixteen-year-olds in the hall where you once fainted in assembly. The headteacher asks you to speak
about "the future of work". The students have a model that writes their essays and a teacher who knows. They look
at you the way you once looked at the people on that stage.

- *Nobody knows* – silence, then one girl claps. Then all of them.
- *What a model cannot do* – they write it down. One of them will remember it in twenty years.
- *Laugh* – they will not remember a word and will remember the afternoon.
- *Decline* – someone from a bank speaks instead. It goes fine.

A child in your life – yours, a neighbour's, a stranger's on a train – asks the question every adult has been
avoiding: "What does it say?"

- *The truth* – "Nobody knows?" "Nobody." A long pause. "Cool."
- *The lighthouse* – you tell it well. It is repeated in a classroom on Monday and from there it goes everywhere.
  Years later you hear your lighthouse in someone else's mouth.

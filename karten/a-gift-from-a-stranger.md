A small box, your name in handwriting, no return address. Inside: a book you once recommended in an interview, with
a note on the first page – "You were right. Thank you." No signature.

- *Pass it on* – you leave a book on a train with a note of your own.
- *Who* – a former worker, a reader, someone you do not remember helping. You write back.
- *Throw it away* – you will never know. You think about it more than you would have.

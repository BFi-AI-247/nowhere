Samir runs the night shift in an emergency room in Marseille and a small telemedicine company in the daytime,
which is why he is always tired and never bored. He believes the models will save more lives than they cost. He
has seen both kinds of night. He is the person the nurses call when the machine says one thing and the patient
says another.

If you play Samir, the Boom is a question of who gets seen first – and who decides.

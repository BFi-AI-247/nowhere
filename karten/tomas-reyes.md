Tomas writes for a paper that still prints, which in 2026 is either a brand or a mistake. He has been right about
three scandals and wrong about one, and the wrong one is what people remember. He believes nothing he is told and
most of what he finds out. He lives alone, well, in a flat with too many books and one window that faces the river.

If you play Tomas, the Boom is a story that is eating the people who could tell it. His own paper will be one of
the first to find out what a model can write.

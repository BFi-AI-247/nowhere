Elena sits in the European Parliament for a district north of Bologna that used to make tiles and now makes
complaints. She was a labour lawyer before that, and she still reads a contract before a speech. She is good on
television and better in a corridor. She has two years until the next election and one idea she has not said out
loud.

If you play Elena, the Boom is a vote she has to cast every month. Everyone will remember which way.

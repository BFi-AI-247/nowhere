A house by water, a boat that works, a village with one shop. For two weeks the Boom is something that happens
to other people.

- *Go* – you come back slower. It lasts a month.
- *Take someone* – the talk happens on the third evening. It needed water.
- *Stay* – you get a lot done and you notice what you did not do.
- *Not this year* – the house by the water is booked by someone else.

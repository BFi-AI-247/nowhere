A lawyer's letter, a name you had to look up, a funeral with nine people. She left you a choice, which is the
most she could have left anyone: the house, the money, or the strip of grass with the hangar.

- *The house* – eleven hectares and no signal. It costs money and gives something else.
- *The money* – clean, simple, gone by autumn.
- *The airfield* – nobody wants it. One day it is the only way to be somewhere first.
- *Her village* – a plaque by the church with her name on it. Not yours.

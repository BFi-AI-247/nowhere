Eleven people, one roast, a cousin who works in a data centre and an uncle who thinks the models are a plot. By the
dessert the table has split into those who are afraid and those who are not yet. Your grandmother asks for the salt.

- *Argue* – you win on points and lose the evening.
- *The food* – it was very good. Everyone agrees on that.
- *The youngest* – she is fourteen and has the clearest view at the table. Nobody else is listening to her.

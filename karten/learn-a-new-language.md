Forty evenings, a teacher who was a diplomat, a language your grandmother spoke. The model translates it
perfectly and you still cannot order bread in it.

- *Finish* – you can order bread. People in that country treat you differently. (In Prototype 1 there is no
  skill gain on a card; the concept lists it – the author gives the trait and the influence instead.)
- *The model* – it translates. You do not learn anything, which you notice later.
- *With a friend* – you learn half as much and laugh twice as often.

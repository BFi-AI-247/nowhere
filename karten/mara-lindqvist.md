Mara owns a mid-sized factory near Łódź that makes the housings nobody notices – for pumps, for charging stations,
for whatever the year wants. She took it over from an uncle who thought she would sell. She did not. She is the
kind of person who reads the fine print at two in the morning and enjoys it. She has never lost money on purpose
and never told the whole truth when half of it would do.

If you play Mara, the Boom is a market first and a question later. Her factory is the thing that can make her rich
and the thing that can make her nobody.

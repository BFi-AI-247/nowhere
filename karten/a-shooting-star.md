Eleven at night, a cold road, no reason to look up, and you do. It is gone before you decide what it was.

- *A wish* – you do not tell anyone. Something in you leans toward believing things.
- *Note it* – it was a Perseid, probably. You look it up. You keep looking things up.
- *Wake someone* – forty minutes in the cold, together. Another one comes. It counts double.

December. The index is where it was in 2025. The models are everywhere and nobody talks about them, the way
nobody talks about electricity. Ninety people, or not. A paper, or not. A factory, a cold store, a gallery, a
parish. The year had five cards a month and you played them.

- *What you kept* – a list. It is shorter than you thought and it is yours.
- *Who you kept* – a shorter list. It is the one that matters.
- *The next thing* – there is always a next thing. That is the whole point of you.

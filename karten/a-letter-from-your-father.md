On paper, in his handwriting, which the model in Grenoble could read and you almost cannot. He has questions
about the year. He has watched it on television. He is not angry. That is new.

- *Write back* – it takes an evening. He reads it twice.
- *Visit* – a weekend. You come back lighter.
- *Not now* – there is always a now that is not this one.
- *Call* – he talks for an hour about the garden. The year is not mentioned. It is in every sentence.

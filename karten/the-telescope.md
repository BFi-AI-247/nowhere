A second-hand telescope from a man who is moving to a flat with no balcony. A tripod, three eyepieces, a book with
a coffee stain on the Pleiades. On the first clear night you point it at a bright dot low in the south, and it has
rings.

- *Saturn* – four neighbours on the balcony at midnight, saying "oh" one after another.
- *The sky* – a winter of cold hands. By spring you know where things are.
- *Someday* – it collects dust and a spider. Someday is a long time.

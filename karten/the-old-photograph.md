It falls out of a book: you, twenty-three, on a beach you cannot place, laughing at something outside the frame
with someone you have not spoken to in fifteen years. On the back, in your handwriting, a date and one word.

- *Call* – the number still works. You both laugh at the same thing again.
- *The fridge* – it watches you make coffee every morning.
- *Restore* – the model fixes the colours and adds a little sky that was not there. You keep the original.

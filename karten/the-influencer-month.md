One post, one night, three hundred thousand people. For a month you are the person they listen to about the
thing that happened to all of them. It will not last. That is the point of it.

- *Sell* – the month pays for itself and then some.
- *Say* – you spend it on something that is not you.
- *Log off* – the quiet is immediate and complete.
- *Keep posting* – it fades in a month, like it was always going to.
- *Someone else* – a food bank, a small band, a junior who lost her job. They get the month.

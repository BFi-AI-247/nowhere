The date is in the calendar and in the model's reminder and somehow still comes as a surprise. There is a person
who has put up with this whole year. There should be a table, and a candle, and you without a phone.

- *The same restaurant* – the waiter is new and the soup is the same.
- *Somewhere new* – a train, a small town, a hotel with a creaking floor.
- *Forget* – the week of apologies is its own kind of anniversary.

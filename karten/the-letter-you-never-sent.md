In a drawer, under the warranty for a kettle you no longer have: a letter you wrote eight years ago to someone you
had a falling-out with and never sent. Your handwriting was angrier then. Most of it is still true.

- *Send it* – a reply comes in a week. Shorter than yours. Enough.
- *Kinder* – you write it again. This one you send. This one gets an answer.
- *Burn it* – a small fire, a glass of wine. Some things are finished by not being said.

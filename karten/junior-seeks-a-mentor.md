He is twenty-seven, clever in a way that is going out of fashion, and he asks – directly, which is rare – whether
you would teach him what you know. He has already learned what the model knows.

- *One hour* – it costs you an hour and gives you someone who tells you what the young think.
- *Bring him in* – he is good with machines. Very.
- *No* – he asks someone else. You notice you said no.
- *Think about it* – he waits a while. Then he asks someone else.
